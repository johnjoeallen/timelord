Weekly schedule

The device may be on during the windows below and is put to sleep or shut down outside them. Each day is its own line from midnight to midnight: a row gives a window on each ticked day, from On from until Off at. Add as many rows as you like, including several for the same day (08:30–10:30 and 18:30–23:30). Windows stay within the day, and Off at 00:00 means midnight at the end of it. Leave On from blank to start at the beginning of the day and Off at blank to run to midnight (both blank is the whole day). A window that ends at midnight carries straight on into the next day's window if that starts at 00:00, so a late night is two rows: Fri on from 18:00 (no off), Sat off at 01:00 (no on from). To be off for a while inside a day, leave a gap: 00:00–16:15 and 16:30–00:00 is off from 16:15 to 16:30. A day with no row is off all day, but a schedule with no rows at all restricts nothing. The line under each row shows its next window. Fill in the bottom row and press + to keep it and get a new blank one (nothing happens if it is empty). − disables a row, which keeps it but ignores it; − on a disabled row deletes it. Nothing changes until you press Save schedule.

Overrides (holidays, one-offs)

An override adds an allowed window on every date in its range, on top of the weekly rows, so it can extend a day (an evening that runs late) but never shorten one. A single day is just a start date with no end date. The window is within the day like a row: On from until Off at, with blank or 00:00 meaning midnight (and a blank On from the start of the day). To run past midnight, add a second override for the next day with no On from. The weekly schedule above is never changed; remove the override and it reverts. Disable keeps an override but stops it adding time, so you can switch a holiday off and back on again (Enable) without re-entering it.
