Weekly schedule

Each rule applies to the evening it starts on: "Fri 01:00" is the night of Friday, so the device goes off at 01:00 on Saturday. An off time earlier than the on time falls after midnight. Every day may appear in at most one rule; clear a rule's days to remove it.

Overrides (holidays, one-offs)

An override replaces the weekly times for every night that starts within its dates. A single day is just a start date with no end date. The weekly schedule above is never changed; remove the override and it reverts.
